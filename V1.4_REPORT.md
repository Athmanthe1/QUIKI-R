## Interpretation choices
"Unknown, not negative": unknown classes get ASL's attenuated, margin-tolerant term; only explicit rejects get a full-strength negative. kNN τ=0.05. Without a fitted stacker the score is a flagged, uncalibrated mean of present scorers. Custom pHash (scipy DCT) replaces `imagehash`; the head ships as torch-free `.npz`.
